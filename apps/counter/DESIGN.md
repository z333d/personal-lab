# Terminal

## Overview

Developer-tool register. Everything is monospace — JetBrains Mono first, system mono fallback. Background is true near-black with a hint of cool blue. The accent is electric lime green, used very sparingly (one button per view, inline keywords).

Tight border-radii (2-4px), no shadows. Borders define structure. Caps + letter-spacing on caption text gives a "system message" feel.

## Don'ts

- ❌ Sans-serif anywhere
- ❌ Drop shadows (terminals are flat)
- ❌ Saturated UI colors except the accent
- ❌ Capital city of celebration animations

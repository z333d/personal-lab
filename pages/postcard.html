This file is one of the starter examples that ship with a fresh lab — a postcard sent from the template to the person opening the door.

Adding a new page is one file in pages/. Adding a new app is one folder in apps/. Deleting either is the inverse. The lab keeps as much of itself as you decide is worth keeping.

When the postcard's job is done, send it on its way: pnpm scaffold rm postcard.

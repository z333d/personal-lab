# Postcard

## Overview

A friendly, postcard register. Cream paper, deep teal ink, bold coral stamp. Display type is a chunky serif (Fraunces) for personality; body is Inter for legibility. Rounded shapes (12-20px) feel approachable, not sharp.

Use the coral accent for primary CTAs and the one thing that should make you smile per page.

## Don'ts

- ❌ Mute the personality with too much hairline structure (this isn't editorial)
- ❌ Pure black or pure gray (use the deep teal as your "black")
- ❌ Tight letter-spacing on body (postcard wants breathing room)
